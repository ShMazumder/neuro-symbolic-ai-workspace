1 Problem & Motivation

Real-time conflict analysis from news text faces a fundamental AI paradox:

  • Neural LLMs / GNNs: Excel at unstructured language but hallucinate, exhibit uncalibrated overconfidence, and lack formal logical guarantees.
  • Symbolic Systems (Rules/Ontologies): Provide auditability and rigor, but shatter under real-world journalistic syntax and lexical noise.
Key Goal: Build a hybrid system grounding live Daily Star news articles in a localized Bangladesh CAMEO knowledge graph with 100% provenance traceability.
2 Bangladesh CAMEO Extension

3 8-Stage NLP Extraction
  • Live News Corpus: 120 Daily Star articles → 53 conflict events → 1,920 RDF triples.
  • Fast Relevance Filter: 50+ BD violence regex keywords + zero-shot BART fallback.
  • Trigger Detection: Lemmatized verbal and nominal lexicons mapping directly to CAMEO roots.
  • Entity & Geo Resolution: spaCy NER resolved against 64 districts & 8 BD divisions.
4 Neuro-Symbolic Architecture

Formal Validation Mechanism: The neural component generates probability logits over CAMEO escalation states. The symbolic engine evaluates constraints:

  • Hard Constraint Violation: Illegal category jumps (e.g. Threaten $\to$ Mass Violence) are intercepted; falls back to safest ancestor code.
  • Soft Constraint Violation: Geographic or actor mismatches incur calibrated confidence penalties.
